1. Introduction and Scope Clause 1.0

This Privacy Policy sets forth the mandatory operational and cryptographic framework by which Votique Governance Systems, Inc. ("Votique", "we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, manages, partitions, and protects data. This document applies uniformly to all authorized participants, including registered electors, nominees, election observers, returning officers, and institutional administrators accessing Votique systems via API, web client, or institutional portals.

By registering, casting an electoral vote, submitting a candidacy nomination dossier, or auditing democratic tally registries on Votique, you acknowledge adherence to the protocols described herein. If an institutional client operates a private tenancy of Votique with custom governance annexes, those supplemental terms are executed in conjunction with, and do not supersede, these core privacy provisions.

2. Information We Collect Clause 2.0

Data ingestion within Votique is strictly limited to verifiable necessity. We distinguish data categories based on the operational role of the participating subject:

2.1 Nominee Dossiers

Individuals standing for elective office, council appointments, or institutional honours provide public-record materials. This includes legal name, designated campaign statements, biographical records, official candidate headshots, and signed affidavits of eligibility. Nominee information is inherently published to the elector roll for voter education upon validation by the designated Returning Board.

2.2 Voter Identity & Elector Verification

To establish eligibility and enforce the "one elector, one vote" democratic premise without compromising secrecy, we collect:

  • Electoral Identifier: Institution-issued student IDs, corporate employee numbers, or national identification credentials provided via SAML/OAuth 2.0 federated directory services.
  • Digital Authentication Factors: Ephemeral cryptographic tokens generated during multi-factor authentication (MFA) challenges.
  • Eligibility Cohorts: Metadata tags indicating voting precinct, academic division, or jurisdictional class to serve accurate ballot templates.

2.3 System Telemetry and Audit Logs

During session activity, our firewalls capture non-nominative technical telemetry: anonymized IP addresses (truncated at the subnet level prior to storage), user-agent strings, timestamp coordinates, and cryptographic handshake latencies. These telemetry logs are physically segregated from ballot records.

3. Anonymity & Cryptographic Ballot Integrity Clause 3.0

Votique implements an uncompromising architectural air-gap between voter authentication and ballot custody:

lock_clock Zero-Knowledge Verifiable Secret Sharing (ZKVSS)

When a voter submits a ballot, the client machine executes a client-side blinding operation. A zero-knowledge cryptographic proof confirms that:

  1. The elector is authorized and has not previously submitted a finalized ballot in this election.
  2. The ballot selections adhere to election parameters (e.g., maximum choices).
  3. No database table, key-value pair, or memory address holds the identity record alongside the unblinded vote.

Once cast, the vote is combined into a homomorphic accumulator or verifiable shuffle mix-net. Even system engineers with physical server access cannot associate an individual elector's identity with their chosen candidate or affirmative/negative decision.

4. How Information is Used and Retained Clause 4.0

Personal and operational data is utilized solely for:

  • Validating the legal electoral franchise of participating accounts.
  • Transmitting cryptographic receipts allowing voters to independently verify their inclusion in the final tally without revealing their vote.
  • Detecting distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) campaigns, automated voter coercion, and fraudulent credential stuffing.

Retention Schedules: In accordance with statutory election preservation mandates, raw authentication traces are purged ninety (90) calendar days following certified contest certification, unless an official judicial recount or election contest petition is served upon Votique. Fully anonymized cryptographic ballot proofs and signed tally hashes remain in public permanence to ensure immutable historical auditability.

5. Third-Party Disclosures & Statutory Compliance Clause 5.0

Votique does not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal information to commercial entities, advertising syndicates, or political campaigns under any circumstance.

Sub-processors and Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud hosting infrastructure is contracted under zero-access data handling agreements with ISO-certified Tier 4 sovereign cloud centers located within the relevant jurisdiction (e.g., EU-GDPR compliant nodes in Frankfurt and Dublin; US-FedRAMP high compliant facilities in Virginia and Oregon).

Subpoenas & Law Enforcement: If compelled by lawful warrant, subpoena, or binding court order, we will disclose only the metadata expressly mandated by judicial decree. Because of our zero-knowledge ballot architecture, Votique cannot be compelled to disclose how any individual voted, as such linkage does not exist in any format.

6. Voter Data Rights & Deletion Procedures Clause 6.0

Under governing data protection frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and statutory Electoral Code provisions), participating electors possess distinct data rights:

Right of Access & Portability:

You may obtain a machine-readable JSON copy of your elector profile, logged participation events, and your non-identifying cryptographic receipt token.

Right of Rectification:

Errors in institutional voter roll eligibility must be rectified via your primary institutional authority prior to poll close. Post-election modifications to cast ballots are mathematically impossible.

Right of Erasure (The "Right to be Forgotten"):

Electors may request the deletion of account records following election finalization. Cast cryptographic tallies cannot be modified or redacted, as doing so would invalidate the mathematical integrity of the entire community vote.

To initiate a statutory rights claim, submit an authenticated petition to privacy@votique.com with your verified institutional domain address.

7. Security Protocols & Breach Notification Clause 7.0

All data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3 with mandatory Perfect Forward Secrecy. Data at rest is encrypted utilizing AES-256-GCM with hardware-security-module (HSM) root key ceremonies. In the improbable event of a validated security incident compromising elector credential caches, Votique will notify affected regulatory supervisory authorities and institutional clients within 72 hours of verification in compliance with legal thresholds.
